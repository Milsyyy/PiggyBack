Update: the nose redness and chest marks come from **squeezing pores**, so rosacea
is less likely. The remaining goals are healing the marks, reducing visible pores
and blackheads, the brow redness, and the dry lips.

## Rule 1: stop squeezing
The "gunk" in nose pores is mostly **sebaceous filaments**. They're normal and
refill in about 30 days. Squeezing causes red marks that last weeks to months
(post-inflammatory erythema), can break small vessels, and pushes bacteria
deeper. Salicylic acid does the job chemically instead.

## How to apply
- Salicylic: a few drops on the nose, chin and forehead (oily areas), not the whole face.
- Azelaic: a pea-sized amount for the whole face. A tingle for 1–2 weeks is normal.
- Retinoid: a pea-sized amount, on dry skin, avoiding the eye corners, nostril
  creases and lips.
- Thin to thick: water-based serums first, then the emulsion or suspension, then
  moisturiser.

## Don't
- Don't use **AHA 30% + BHA 2% Peeling Solution** (the red one). It's too strong
  while the marks are healing.
- Don't use **Vitamin C Suspension 23%**. It stings on reactive skin.
- Don't put the retinoid and salicylic on the same night.
- Introduce one new product every 1–2 weeks, so you know what's responsible if
  something reacts.

## Expect
- Squeeze marks fade over 4–12 weeks.
- Blackheads and pores improve in 6–12 weeks.
- Retinoid benefits show at 3–6 months.

Stop the active and go back to cleanse + moisturise if you get burning, peeling
or redness that lasts more than a day.
