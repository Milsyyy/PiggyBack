# Routine: The Ordinary-based

Update: the nose redness and chest marks come from **squeezing pores**, so rosacea
is less likely. The remaining goals are healing the marks, reducing visible pores
and blackheads, the brow redness, and the dry lips.

## Rule 1: stop squeezing
The "gunk" in nose pores is mostly **sebaceous filaments**. They're normal and
refill in about 30 days. Squeezing causes red marks that last weeks to months
(post-inflammatory erythema), can break small vessels, and pushes bacteria
deeper. Salicylic acid does the job chemically instead.

## Products (The Ordinary + 2 outside)
| Role | Product |
|---|---|
| Cleanser | Squalane Cleanser *or* Glucoside Foaming Cleanser |
| Moisturiser | Natural Moisturizing Factors + HA (or + Beta Glucan) |
| Pores / blackheads | Salicylic Acid 2% Solution (or 2% Anhydrous) |
| Red marks / redness | Azelaic Acid 10% Suspension |
| Oil / pores (optional) | Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% |
| Retinoid (later) | Granactive Retinoid 2% Emulsion → Retinal 0.2% Emulsion |
| Sunscreen | Any SPF 30–50 you'll wear daily (TO's UV Filters SPF 45 Serum is fine) |
| *Outside TO* | Ketoconazole 2% shampoo (brows, beard, chest) · plain petrolatum for lips |

## Daily
**AM:** cleanse (or water) → Niacinamide (optional) → moisturiser → **SPF, ½ tsp face + neck**

**PM:** cleanse → *tonight's active* (see the schedule) → moisturiser → petrolatum on lips

## Weekly PM schedule

**Weeks 1–2:** cleanse + moisturiser only, plus ketoconazole. This lets the
squeeze marks start healing.

**Weeks 3–8:**
| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salicylic | Azelaic | Rest | Salicylic | Azelaic | Rest | Azelaic |

**Week 9+**, if your skin is calm, add a retinoid:
| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retinoid | Salicylic | Azelaic | Retinoid | Rest | Salicylic | Azelaic |

**Ketoconazole 2% shampoo, 2–3×/week in the shower:** brows, between the brows,
the sides of the nose, the beard and the chest. Leave for 3–5 minutes, then rinse.
Drop to once a week once the brows are clear.

**Chest:** Salicylic 2% on the chest on salicylic nights, plus the ketoconazole wash.

## How to apply
- Salicylic: a few drops on the nose, chin and forehead (oily areas), not the whole face.
- Azelaic: a pea-sized amount for the whole face. A tingle for 1–2 weeks is normal.
- Retinoid: a pea-sized amount, on dry skin, avoiding the eye corners, nostril
  creases and lips.
- Thin to thick: water-based serums first, then the emulsion or suspension, then
  moisturiser.

## Don't
- Don't use **AHA 30% + BHA 2% Peeling Solution** (the red one). It's too strong
  while the marks are healing.
- Don't use **Vitamin C Suspension 23%**. It stings on reactive skin.
- Don't put the retinoid and salicylic on the same night.
- Introduce one new product every 1–2 weeks, so you know what's responsible if
  something reacts.

## Expect
- Squeeze marks fade over 4–12 weeks.
- Blackheads and pores improve in 6–12 weeks.
- Retinoid benefits show at 3–6 months.

Stop the active and go back to cleanse + moisturise if you get burning, peeling
or redness that lasts more than a day.
